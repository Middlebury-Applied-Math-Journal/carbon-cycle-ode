# Analyzing Our Impact on the Carbon Cycle Through Ordinary Diferential Equations

Carbon dioxide acts as a greenhouse gas when stored in the atmosphere. The burning of fossil fuels and biomass upsets the natural balance of the carbon cycle by releasing long-ago stored carbon into the air, tipping the equilibrium towards the atmospheric storage. A model consisting of a system of ordinary di!erential equations allows for examination of the dynamics of the carbon cycle and how humans have im- pacted it. The results show that no ‘tipping point’ point ex- ists, past which human involvement may push the system far enough that it could not recover. However, fossil fuel use still spikes atmospheric CO2 to dangerous levels, and energy from biomass raises the steady state value of atmospheric CO2. The paper concludes that human involvement tips the carbon cycle towards the atmospheric storage, contributing to the green- house e!ect and warming the planet. Furthermore, biomass cannot truly be considered an environmentally-friendly solu- tion because it too contributes heavily to atmospheric carbon. Lastly, reforestation is presented as a possible solution, which variation of the plant assimilation parameter shows to be ef- fective in reducing atmospheric CO2.
